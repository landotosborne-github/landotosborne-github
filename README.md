### Hi, I'm Landon Osborne 👋

Physics-trained hardware R&D engineer based in Tacoma, WA. I build RF test systems and take hardware from concept to release. Most recently at MOS Equipment (Mission Darkness), I built **SKIPPY**, an automated 100 MHz – 6 GHz shielding-effectiveness test bench, and led Faraday enclosure products through launch.

Open to RF test, hardware and test-automation roles in the Greater Seattle area.
